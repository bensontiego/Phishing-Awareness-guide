# What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of social engineering attack whereby criminals pretend to be a trusted person or organization to trick you into giving away sensitive information, clicking a malicious link or opening an infected attachment.

## Common types

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Email phishing | Mass emails that imitate banks, delivery companies or IT teams | "Your account will be suspended - verify now" |
| Spear phishing | Targeted emails using personal or workplace details | A fake message from your manager asking for a document |
| Smishing | Phishing by SMS | "Your parcel is held - pay the fee here" |
| Vishing | Phishing by phone call | A caller claiming to be from the bank's fraud team |
| Clone phishing | A genuine email is copied and the link replaced with a malicious one | A recent "updated" invoice |

## What attackers want

- Usernames and passwords
- Banking and card details
- Personal information about identity theft
- Access to install malware or ransomware

## Why it works

Attackers rely on **urgency, fear, curiosity and authority** to make people act before they think.
